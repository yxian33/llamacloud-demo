The chart displays Forward P/E ratios over a 9-year period from February 2014 to February 2023. The data shows:

- **Feb 2016 - Feb 2018**: Gradual upward trend, reaching levels consistently above the mean (15.3)
- **Feb 2018 - Feb 2020**: Continued elevation with ratios frequently between 16-17, approaching the +1SD line
- **Feb 2020**: Sharp spike reaching nearly 19.5, well above the +1SD threshold of 16.7
- **Feb 2020 - Feb 2021**: Dramatic decline from the peak, falling below the mean to around 13-15 range
- **Feb 2021 - Feb 2023**: Sustained decline with ratios consistently below the mean, ending near 13.5 and approaching the -1SD line of 14.0
