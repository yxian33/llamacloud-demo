* **Confidence in further disinflation:** We still expect below-consensus inflation for some economies this year, but the picture for 2025 is more mixed.

* **Big downward revision in China and Europe to undershoot:** We cut headline inflation to a below-consensus -0.1% from 1.5% for 2024 and to 1.2% from 1.7% for 2025 on the back of lower food and core prices. Flattered by falling energy prices, eurozone inflation will drop temporarily below the ECB's 2% target during 2024, we expect. Our forecasts for 2025 see it settling at about 2%.

* **EM disinflation:** We continue to believe that all major EM central banks are set to reach inflation targets by Q4 2025, but we expect some re-acceleration before then, most notably in the CE3.

* **US and Japan outliers with upside risk to consensus:** We revise up our US inflation profile, now seeing the end-2025 headline rate at a still-above-target 2.6% (the current Bloomberg consensus is 2.3%). For Japan, we see inflation stable at around 2.0% over 2024-25, versus the 2025 consensus of 1.7%.
