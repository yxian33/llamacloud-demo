## Regional Classifications

- **Highly geared to China**: Countries with high positive sensitivity to China's market movements in both directions
- **Low sensitivity to China**: Countries with minimal correlation to China's market performance
- **Defensive to China**: Countries that tend to move opposite to China's market direction
- **Asymmetric risks to China**: Countries that show different sensitivities depending on whether China's market is up or down
