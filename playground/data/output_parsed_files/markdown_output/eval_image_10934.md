# Central banks: Overview

## US Federal Reserve

**BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts**

| End-2024   | End-2025   |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| 4.50-4.75% | 3.75-4.00% |


**Current policy settings:**
- Policy rate: 5.25-5.50%
- Balance sheet: Rundown USD95bn/month in Treasuries and MBS
- Other measures: Standing repo facility

While we expect positive supply shocks to remain a significant part of the US growth story, the Fed will not be able to draw this conclusion confidently in real time. As a result, we think the central bank will adopt a careful approach to lowering the policy rate.

Strong growth, low unemployment, and improving albeit still-above-target inflation should translate into fewer cuts and a slightly later start than we had expected previously.

We now expect the first cut in June 2024, with 75bp of cuts both this year and next year. This suggests an end-2025 fed funds rate of 4.00% – in our view, still above neutral, implying further cuts in 2026.

## European Central Bank

**BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts**

| End-2024 | End-2025 |
| -------- | -------- |
| 3.00%    | 2.50%    |


**Current policy settings:**
- Policy rate: 4.50% (deposit rate)
- Asset purchases: Full PEPP reinvestment in H1, stepping down to around 50% reinvestment in H2, concluding at end-2024.

We recently revised our forecast of the start of the ECB cutting cycle to June from April. Signs of recovery in economic activity have afforded the Governing Council more time to evaluate incoming data, especially on wages.

The later start to the cutting cycle means we expect 100bp of cuts this year (compared with 125bp previously), but we still see the Deposit Facility Rate reaching 2.50% in June 2025, where we expect it to settle.

Elsewhere, we expect the ECB to use its Operational Framework Review to announce a gradual transition to a demand-driven floor system for steering short-term interest rates.

## People's Bank of China

**BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts**

| End-2024 | End-2025 |
| -------- | -------- |
| 2.25%    | 2.15%    |


**Current policy settings:**
- Policy rate: 2.50% (MLF rate)
- Other measures: RMB4.3trn relending programme and RMB3.3trn PSL as of December 2023

The PBoC will continue to ease policy, we expect, due to downward pressure on the economy and deflation risks. We expect it to cut its policy rate by 10bp in Q2 – as the February cut was limited to the five-year LPR – followed by another 15bp cut in Q3 after the Fed is likely to have started its easing cycle.

As a secular trend, we expect another 25bp cut to the required reserve ratio in H2 to inject long-term liquidity into the banking system.

The PBoC is likely to keep using relending programmes and pledged supplementary lending to ramp up support to policy-favoured areas (e.g. public housing).

## Bank of Japan

**BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts**

| End-2024               | End-2025               |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 0.25% (IOER)           | 0.75% (IOER)           |
| 10y: up to about 1.25% | 10y: up to about 1.75% |


**Current policy settings:**
- Policy rate: -0.1% (IOER);
- 10y: up to about 1%
- Asset purchases: JGBs unlimited for YCC; ETFs ≤JPY180bn/year; CP and corporate bond holdings to be reduced to pre-Covid levels

The BoJ will end its negative interest rate policy this spring (most likely in March) and set a 0–0.1% target range for the overnight rate, we expect. We think it will abandon its commitment to keep expanding base money.

We expect the BoJ to seek a smooth exit by keeping a loose cap on the 10y rate and reiterating that it sees monetary conditions staying accommodative for some time.

That said, we expect inflation to settle at about 2% y/y and the BoJ to hike every six months or so. We see the policy rate at 0.25% by end-2024 and 0.75% by end-2025, with risk skewed to the upside.
