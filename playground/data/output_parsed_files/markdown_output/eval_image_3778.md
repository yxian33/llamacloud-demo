* **Slower wage growth still holds key to 'last mile'**: Although falling, services prices in most economies are still running well above their pre-Covid trends.

* **Stronger evidence of lower expectations**: Lower headline inflation usually drives down inflation expectations and thus wage demands. In both the US and eurozone, we think wage growth has passed its peak.

* **More rebalancing of job market**: Although the labour market in the US has cooled, it remains tight historically, judging by the ratio between job openings and the unemployment rate.

In the US, this rebalancing should be driven by growth in the labour force linked to higher immigration, helped to some extent by productivity gains.

In the eurozone, employment growth should slow, after a period of significant resilience despite weak GDP growth.
