## Chart Description
The chart displays the USD-NOK Real Effective Exchange Rate from 2000 to 2024, indexed to January 1999 = 100. The REER shows significant volatility over the period:

- **2000-2002**: Initial rise from around 105 to peaks near 125-127
- **2002-2008**: Decline to lows around 75-80
- **2008-2016**: Gradual recovery with fluctuations between 80-120
- **2016-2020**: Sharp rise to around 140
- **2020-2022**: Extreme volatility with peaks exceeding 150
- **2022-2024**: Sharp decline to current levels around 145

The gray shaded area represents various averages of the REER, providing context for the current exchange rate relative to historical norms. The current estimate shows the rate is positioned within the upper portion of the historical average range.
