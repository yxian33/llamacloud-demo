

**Farm**

Sludge byproducts (biosolids) from wastewater treatment plants are spread on agricultural land as fertilizer and can contain PFAS and lead to water and food contamination.

Food products, such as milk, can become contaminated if livestock consume PFAS in food or water.

**Manufacturing site**

Manufacturing sites can contaminate ground or surface waters with PFAS, or emit PFAS into the air where it can be inhaled or fall onto surfaces, even far from point sources, and can contaminate soil and bodies of water.

**Airport**

Groundwater and surface water can be contaminated when firefighting foam is used at civilian and military airports or PFAS-containing products are disposed of in landfills.

**Wastewater treatment plant**

Wastewater treatment plants can discharge PFAS into source waters used by drinking water systems.

**Public drinking water system**

Drinking water can contain PFAS, if contaminated source water is untreated.

**Private well**

Private wells can be contaminated with groundwater containing PFAS.

**Residential area**

Consumer products (e.g., carpet, food packaging, and non-stick cookware) can contain PFAS, which can be inhaled or ingested.
