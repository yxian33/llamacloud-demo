# Japan: BoJ to gradually normalise as inflation becomes entrenched

Inflation is likely to fall in H2 2024 as goods price inflation wanes, but strengthening wage pressures should keep services inflation elevated

Wage growth picked up last year and will likely rise further in 2024 as living costs continue to creep up and the labour market remains very tight

* The economic recovery will resume, we expect, after being held back in Q1 2024 by the impact of a big earthquake and production disruptions in the auto sector. We believe the main problem for the economy has already shifted from deficient demand to supply-side constraints arising from labour shortages. With profits remaining high, corporates' appetite for capex remains strong.

* We expect underlying inflation pressure to build even as the impact of yen depreciation wanes. A cycle of inflation and wage growth now seems to be re-established, with this year's pay talks set to result in another solid rise.

* Fiscal policy is set to remain loose: the government plans a temporary tax cut for this year and progressive rises in defence and childcare spending.

* We do not think Fed rate cuts will preclude gradual policy normalisation by the Bank of Japan, if the global economy holds up as we expect. We expect the BoJ to end its negative interest rate policy this spring and then hike roughly every six months as inflation settles around 2%.
