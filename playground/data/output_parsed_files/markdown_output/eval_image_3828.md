• In Europe, real incomes look set to keep growing. Real wages are improving, reflecting tight labour markets and resilient nominal wage growth, as well as decelerating inflation. As a result, the outlook for consumption is brightening.

• In the US, we think the consumer outlook remains optimistic, as a tight labour market and abating inflation support personal income growth. An estimated increase in taxes paid during the 2024 filing season points to moderation in the pace of growth in after-tax inflation-adjusted income.

• A USD12trn increase in household net worth in 2023, driven by real estate and equity values, will also likely support solid US consumer spending in 2024.

• Housing prices are rising due to a stubbornly limited housing stock, while equity prices have reflected consensus for a more positive economic outlook. Consumers have significantly reduced debt burdens since the 2008-09 recession. The ratio of household net worth to liabilities shows the financial backdrop remains favourable for consumers to drive economic growth in 2024.
