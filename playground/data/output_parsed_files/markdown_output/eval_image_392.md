**Chart Description:**
This dual-axis line chart shows the relationship between US Consumer Price Index (CPI) year-over-year percentage change (teal line, left axis) and PMI Composite Output Prices with a 5-month lead (orange line, right axis) from February 2014 to February 2024. The chart demonstrates significant correlation between the two measures, particularly during the 2021-2022 inflationary period where both metrics peaked simultaneously - US CPI reaching approximately 9.1% and PMI Output Prices reaching around 75.0. Both measures show a decline from their peaks toward the end of the time series.
