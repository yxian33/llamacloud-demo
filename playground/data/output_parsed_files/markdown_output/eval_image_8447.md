

# VenusTech Company Timeline

**1996**
Dr. Yan Wang Jia founds VenusTech

**2000**
Launched world's first hardware IDS product

Became one of the first 15 companies with "Grade A Qualification of the Integration of the Confidential Information Systems" in China

**2005**

**2010**
Listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, and became the first "nationally recognized enterprise technology center" for information security in China

Merged with Leadsec (previously Lenovo Information Security) to become China's largest network security vendor

**2011**

**2014**
Acquired Beijing Shusheng Electronics Technology Co., Ltd, and Hangzhou United Information Technology

**2015**
IDS /IPS, VPN, UTM & firewall product ranges achieved No.1 market-share in IDC and CCID 2015 report

**2017**
Launched commercial SaaS product - YunZi trusted security platform; Chengdu Security Operations Center starts operations

**2019**
Became the only security company that participated in the entire drafting process of "classified information protection 2.0".

**2022**
The company established a strategic partnership with China Mobile Group, which has now become the actual controller and largest shareholder of VenusTech
