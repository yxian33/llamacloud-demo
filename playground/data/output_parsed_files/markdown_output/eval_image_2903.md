
# PFAS Drinking Water Standards by State

This map shows the adoption of PFAS (Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) drinking water standards across the United States, with different colors and patterns indicating the level of standards each state has adopted.

## Legend

|   | States that have adopted a standard lower than 70 ppt  |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------ |
|   | States that have adopted a standard equal to 70 ppt    |
|   | States that have adopted a standard higher than 70 ppt |
|   | 19 Individual PFAS Standards                           |
|   | States that have not regulated PFAS in drinking water  |


The map displays the continental United States with an inset showing Alaska and Hawaii. States are colored according to their PFAS regulation status, with many states shown in gray (no regulation), several northeastern and some western states shown in dark red (standards lower than 70 ppt), a few states in orange (70 ppt standard), and some states with diagonal blue and red striped patterns indicating standards higher than 70 ppt or individual PFAS standards.
