
# Commodity Market Positioning Chart

## Market Conditions and Price Momentum

The chart displays commodities positioned across four market condition quadrants with directional price momentum indicators:

**Market Quadrants:**
- **Deficit** (top)
- **Surplus** (bottom) 
- **Tightening** (left)
- **Loosening** (right)

**Price Momentum:**
- Positive price momentum (diagonal arrow from bottom-left to top-right)
- Negative price momentum (diagonal arrow from top-left to bottom-right)
