• **Positive supply dynamics:** Our upgrade of growth forecasts for advanced economies reflects a more optimistic assessment of global supply dynamics.

• In the US, productivity and the labour force already caught up with pre-Covid levels last year. We expect this positive trend to continue, at least in 2024. In particular, the latest CBO projections suggest that undocumented immigration could boost labour force growth by 1pp in 2024, implying supply growth closer to 3% y/y than the commonly used 2% estimates.

• In Europe, natural gas prices have fallen more than we expected and are now back to their pre-pandemic range. It takes time for gas prices to affect the economy; but after five quarters of stagnation, we think the European economy will soon benefit from this positive supply shock.

• While continued excess capacity in China's industrial sector is a cause for concern for its own economy, it is a source of disinflation for the world, and thus good news in the short run for advanced economies.
