
# La Niña

During La Niña conditions, the following patterns occur across the Pacific Ocean:

## Regional Effects

**South East Asia and Australia:**
- Increased convection
- Increased rain
- Warmer than normal temperatures

**Americas:**
- Cooler than normal temperatures

## Atmospheric Circulation

- Strong trade winds blow from east to west across the Pacific Ocean

## Oceanic Circulation

- Cold water pressing upwards, replacing the warm surface water
- Enhanced upwelling occurs along the eastern Pacific, bringing cold, nutrient-rich water to the surface
- Warm surface waters are pushed westward toward Asia and Australia
- The thermocline (boundary between warm surface water and cold deep water) becomes more pronounced, with warm water concentrated in the western Pacific and cold water dominating the eastern Pacific
