**Legend:**
- **Fw. P/E (x)**: Forward Price-to-Earnings ratio (red line)
- **3-year mean**: Historical average (gray line)
- **+1STD**: Plus one standard deviation (gray line)
- **+2STD**: Plus two standard deviations (black line)
- **-1STD**: Minus one standard deviation (gray line)
- **-2STD**: Minus two standard deviations (black line)

**Key Observations:**
- The Forward P/E ratio shows significant volatility over the period
- A notable sharp decline occurred in early 2018, reaching the lowest point around 12x
- The ratio remained relatively stable in the 15-17 range through most of 2018
- A dramatic spike occurred at the end of the period, reaching approximately 35x
- The statistical bands provide context for normal trading ranges versus extreme movements
