• **Robust US growth in sight:** Following a stronger-than-expected 2023, we revise up our US GDP growth estimate to 2.8% y/y for 2024, and we now expect 1.8% for 2025 (up from 2.0% and 1.4%, respectively).

• A positive supply shock will continue at least for this year, we estimate, allowing stronger growth without inflation accelerating.

• The Fed will move slowly, we think, as it seeks evidence that stronger activity will not be inflationary, and our forecast adjustments imply a shallower cutting path than we previously envisioned (see page 24).

• **Europe to join the party:** Our GDP forecasts for the eurozone remain broadly unchanged relative to our Global Outlook 2024. However, we now think risks are balanced rather than tilted to the downside.

• We still expect a gradual recovery, thanks to the continued improvement in real incomes and the normalisation of borrowing costs.

• Thanks to the NextGenerationEU recovery fund, the periphery will continue outperforming core economies, in our view, especially in the near term.
