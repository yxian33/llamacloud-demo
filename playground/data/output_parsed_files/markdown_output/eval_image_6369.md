## Self-identifying comments:

**Williams** - 3 rate cuts in 2024 "reasonable starting point" (28 Feb)

**Mester** - 3 rate cuts "feels about right to me" (29 Feb)

**Bostic** - Expects 2 rate cuts this year (16 Feb)

**Daly** - Median forecast of 3 rate cuts this year is a "reasonable baseline" (16 Feb)

**Kashkari** - Currently thinking two cuts, potentially one for 2024 (6 Mar)

## Other comments:

**Powell** - "When we do get that confidence -- and we're not far from it -- it will be appropriate to dial back that level of restriction." (7 Mar)

**Bowman** - It's "not yet" time to lower rates (7 Mar)

**Kugler** - "At some point, the continued cooling of inflation and labor markets may make it appropriate to reduce the target range for the federal funds rate." (7 Feb)

**Waller** - "My conjecture is that, in the absence of a major economic shock, delaying rate cuts by a few months should not have a substantial impact on the real economy in the near term." (22 Feb)

**Barkin** - CPI data confirms why Fed needs more confidence to cut (16 Feb)

**Goolsbee** - Don't support waiting until inflation at 2% for cut (14 Feb)

**Collin** - Believe Fed easing will be appropriate later this year (28 Feb)

**Schmid** - "My own view is that there is no need to preemptively adjust the stance of policy." (26 Feb)

**Harker** - Close to cutting, just give us a "couple meetings" (22 Feb)

**Logan** - "I'm not really seeing any urgency to make any additional adjustments" (9 Feb)
