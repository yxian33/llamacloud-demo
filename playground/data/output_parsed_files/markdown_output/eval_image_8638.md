**Key Observations:**
- The Generic TRx maintained relatively stable levels around 850,000-900,000 from April 2021 through October 2022
- A brief peak of approximately 950,000 was reached in late 2022
- A significant downward trend began in late 2022/early 2023, indicated by the blue arrow
- By January 2024, values had declined to approximately 400,000, representing more than a 50% decrease from peak levels
