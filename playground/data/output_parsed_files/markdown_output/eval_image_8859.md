# Central banks: Overview

## Bank of England

| BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts | BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| End-2024                          | End-2025                          |
| 4.25%                             | 3.25%                             |


**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 5.25%
Other measures: Active gilt sales

We think the BoE will begin its rate-cutting cycle in June as inflation pressures ease. We expect four consecutive 25bp cuts in 2024.

As growth picks up, the BoE is likely to slow its pace to one cut per quarter in 2025. Bank Rate will remain restrictive over 2025 and end the year at 3.25%, we expect, 25bp above our previous forecast.

**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 5.75%

We expect the NBP to keep rates on hold until it resumes its easing cycle in Q4, premised on core inflation moderating. Our base case is for a cut in November.

There is significant scope for further easing in 2025, in our view, with risks skewed to a lower terminal rate than our 4% forecast.

| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| End-2024                          | End-2025                          |
| 9.00%                             | 9.00%                             |


**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 11.25%
Other measures: None

We expect the BCB to maintain a 50bp-per-meeting pace of cuts in 2024 until a final 25bp cut in September. We expect it to show some caution due to high and volatile global rates, as well as local factors.

Inflation and inflation expectations above target, combined with looser fiscal policy, are likely to require the BCB to maintain high rates in 2025.

## Bank of Mexico

| BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts | BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| End-2024                          | End-2025                          |
| 9.50%                             | 7.50%                             |


**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 11.25%

We expect Banxico to follow a cautious and gradual easing cycle as ongoing disinflation still faces upside risks. We anticipate a return to a neutral rate of about 6.00% by 2026.

Banxico's reaction function is likely to consider not only inflation dynamics but also factors such as fiscal policy, the economic cycle and the Fed's monetary policy stance, increasing the risk of a discontinuous cutting path.

## South African Reserve Bank

| BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts | BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| End-2024                          | End-2025                          |
| 7.25%                             | 7.00%                             |


**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 8.25%
Other measures: None

The SARB will start cutting rates in May, we expect, as we see inflation falling to its 4.5% midpoint target already by early Q3. However, potential ZAR volatility linked to the general election, close to May's policy meeting, could make the SARB hesitate and move later.

We expect 125bp of cuts in this cycle, 25bp less than before due to higher US fed funds and likely higher cyclical r-star globally.

## Bank of Korea

| BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts | BNP Paribas policy-rate forecasts |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| End-2024                          | End-2025                          |
| 2.75%                             | 2.50%                             |


**Current policy settings**
Policy rate: 3.50%

Our assessment of the domestic economy underpins our unchanged view that the BoK will start to ease monetary policy in May, with three 25bp rate cuts in 2024 and another 25bp cut in 2025, lowering the policy rate to 2.50%.

Risks remain tilted to the cycle not starting until Q3, in our view, which should mean fewer moves in 2024 and more cuts in 2025.
