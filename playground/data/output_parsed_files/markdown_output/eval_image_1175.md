* **Key elections**: The main political events in Europe this year are elections. Voting for the European Parliament is on 6–9 June, and we expect the UK general election in Q4 (the election has yet to be called but must be held by 28 January 2025). We will also closely watch regional elections in Germany in the autumn, as they will test mainstream parties' resistance to collaborating with the far-right AfD ahead of the federal election, expected in autumn 2025.

* **Continuity and change**: For the European Parliament, opinion polls point to more political fragmentation under unchanged leadership. But in the UK, indications are that the Labour Party will win power after 14 years in opposition.

* **Implications**: With fiscal headroom limited across Europe, we expect the elections to change policy priorities rather than result in outright fiscal stimulus. In the EU, defence and security initiatives are likely to become more prioritised, representing a slight shift away from the green agenda. In the UK, we expect a Labour government to undertake a redistribution-focused agenda.
