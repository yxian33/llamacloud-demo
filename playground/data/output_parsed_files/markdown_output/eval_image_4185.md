

## Medium-term outlook
Looking beyond 2025, we estimate that trend growth is around 1.3%, relatively stable from the past decade. We estimate that the output gap will be slightly negative in 2024 and assume 1.4% GDP growth for 2025-27.

## View from international organisations
The European Commission and the IMF estimate potential growth at 1.2% and 1.3%, respectively – the same level as in 2005-15 and a moderate rebound from the 2015-22 period.

## Investment uptrend
French policies have been pro-business since 2014 and the creation of the tax credit for competitiveness by President Hollande. President Macron extended these policies with cuts in labour, production and corporate taxes. One of the successes of these policies has been to raise business investment trends. As a share of GDP, investment in machinery and IP products has increased by 3pp in a decade and is now the highest among European peers.

Attractiveness has also improved. According to the Ernst & Young yearly survey, France has been a leader in FDI in Europe since 2019. Higher investment rates should help modernise production capacities, supporting potential growth.
