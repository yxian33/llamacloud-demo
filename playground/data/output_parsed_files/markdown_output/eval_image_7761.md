**Chart Legend:**
- **2Y EUR** (Orange line): 2-Year Euro swap spread
- **10Y EUR** (Black line): 10-Year Euro swap spread  
- **2Y USD** (Gray line): 2-Year USD swap spread
- **10Y USD** (Red line): 10-Year USD swap spread

**Y-axis:** Swap spread (bp) - ranging from -60 to 100 basis points
**X-axis:** Time period from 2017 to 2024

**Key Observations:**
- Euro swap spreads (both 2Y and 10Y) peaked during the SVB crisis in early 2023
- USD swap spreads remained negative throughout most of the period
- Significant volatility occurred around major economic events including COVID-19, Fed policy changes, and geopolitical events
