* Although PMI surveys remain at levels consistent with a contraction, their recent rebound gives us confidence that an economic recovery is underway.

* GDP growth will gradually strengthen from 0.1% q/q in Q1 to 0.4% in Q4 2024, we forecast, stabilising at this level in 2025. We continue to expect domestic demand to be the main driver of the recovery, with real incomes set to keep rising and the monetary stance to start normalising from June.

* While we think the economic backdrop will gradually improve across the eurozone, the periphery's post-pandemic outperformance will continue, partly because of the NextGenerationEU recovery package. Core economies should gradually benefit from exports, but domestic demand there looks set to be weakened by both fiscal consolidation and sluggish private consumption in the near term.

* We see upside risks from a faster-than-expected normalisation of savings rates. Potential trade tensions between the US and the eurozone pose a downside risk to our forecast in the medium term (see page 32).
