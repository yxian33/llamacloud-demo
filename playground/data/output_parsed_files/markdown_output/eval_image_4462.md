

# SOI Wafer Manufacturing Process

## Process Flow Steps

**1. Initial silicon**
- Starting wafer A (blue) and wafer B (light blue/transparent)

**2. Oxidation**
- Wafer A develops an oxide layer (shown in green)

**3. Implantation**
- Ion implantation process applied to wafer A (indicated by black arrows)

**4. Cleaning and bonding**
- Wafer A and wafer B are cleaned and bonded together

**5. Splitting**
- The bonded wafer assembly is split along the implanted layer

**6. Annealing and CMP touch polishing**
- Final processing steps to create the finished SOI wafer

**7. Donor wafer becomes new wafer A**
- The process yields an SOI wafer and a donor wafer that can be recycled as a new wafer A for subsequent processing

## Final Products
- **SOI wafer**: Silicon-On-Insulator wafer ready for use
- **Recycled donor wafer**: Can be reused in the process as wafer A
