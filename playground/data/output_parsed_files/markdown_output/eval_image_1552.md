**Y-axis:** Swap spread (bp) ranging from -75 to 60 basis points

**Key Observations:**
- The 2Y EUR:EZ spread showed the most dramatic movement, spiking to around 60bp during the SVB crisis in early 2023
- Both 10Y spreads (USD and EUR:EZ) ended at similar levels around -37bp by 2024
- COVID-19 caused significant disruption across all spreads in 2020
- The Russia-Ukraine invasion and subsequent ECB hiking cycle had notable impacts on European spreads
