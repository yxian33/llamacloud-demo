

# Central Region Footprint on National Gas Grid

**GAIL** (गेल)

This map displays the gas pipeline network infrastructure across India, showing the Central Region's footprint on the National Gas Grid operated by GAIL (Gas Authority of India Limited).

## Geographic Coverage

The network spans across the following states and regions:

**Northern Region:**
- Ladakh (Leh)
- Jammu & Kashmir (Jammu)
- Himachal Pradesh
- Punjab
- Haryana (New Delhi region)
- Uttar Pradesh
- Uttarakhand

**Western Region:**
- Rajasthan
- Gujarat (Dahej, Hazira)
- Maharashtra (Dabhol, Vijaipur)

**Central Region:**
- Madhya Pradesh
- Chhattisgarh

**Eastern Region:**
- Bihar
- Jharkhand
- West Bengal
- Odisha (Dhamra)

**Southern Region:**
- Telangana
- Andhra Pradesh
- Karnataka
- Tamil Nadu (Ennore)
- Kerala (Kochi)

## Pipeline Network

The map shows an extensive network of gas pipelines represented by:
- **Green lines**: Primary pipeline routes connecting major hubs
- **Black lines**: Secondary and regional distribution networks

The network forms a comprehensive grid connecting:
- Northern terminals and distribution points
- Western coastal facilities (Dahej, Hazira, Dabhol)
- Central distribution hubs
- Eastern coastal terminals (Dhamra)
- Southern terminals (Ennore, Kochi)

The pipeline infrastructure creates an integrated national gas grid enabling gas distribution from production and import terminals to consumption centers across the country.
