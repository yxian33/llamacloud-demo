
# Economic Trends: Net Interest Payments and Fed Funds Rate (1984-2023)

| Period    | Net Interest Payments (Billions $) | Fed Funds Rate (%) | Economic Conditions                       |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1984-1985 | \~95-110                           | \~8-11%            | Recession period                          |
| 1986-1987 | \~110-170                          | \~6-8%             | Rising interest payments, declining rates |
| 1988-1990 | \~170-195                          | \~6-10%            | Peak rates around 10%                     |
| 1990-1991 | \~195-120                          | \~10-3%            | Recession period, sharp rate decline      |
| 1992-1999 | \~120-220                          | \~3-6%             | Gradual increase in payments              |
| 2000-2001 | \~220-160                          | \~6-1%             | Recession period, rate cuts               |
| 2002-2006 | \~160-330                          | \~1-5%             | Sharp increase in interest payments       |
| 2007-2009 | \~330-300                          | \~5-0%             | Recession period, rates near zero         |
| 2010-2015 | \~300-350                          | \~0-0.5%           | Extended low rate period                  |
| 2016-2019 | \~350-380                          | \~0.5-2.5%         | Gradual rate increases                    |
| 2020      | \~380-310                          | \~2.5-0%           | Recession period, emergency rate cuts     |
| 2021-2023 | \~310-180                          | \~0-5.5%           | Rapid rate increases, declining payments  |


**Key Observations:**
- Gray shaded areas indicate recession periods (early 1990s, 2001, 2007-2009, 2020)
- Blue arrows in the chart highlight significant upward trends in Net Interest Payments
- Net Interest Payments peaked around $380 billion in 2020
- Fed Funds Rate reached historic lows near 0% during 2008-2015 and 2020-2021
- Recent period shows inverse relationship: rising Fed Funds Rate (to ~5.5%) with declining Net Interest Payments (to ~$180 billion)
- The chart spans from 1984 to 2023, showing approximately 40 years of economic data
