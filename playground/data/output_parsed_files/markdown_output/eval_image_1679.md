• We expect the unusually high degree of synchronisation across central bank policy cycles to continue during 2024-25.

• Rather than a deliberate coordination of policy responses, it reflects the importance of global factors in driving the inflation surge, with those factors now unwinding.

• Illustrating this synchronicity, we now expect the Fed, ECB and BoE to all start their easing cycles in June.

• We expect the mantra from DM central banks when the cutting cycle begins to be one of policy 'normalisation'.

• This implies that central banks will aim to take rates back to neutral, rather than go below it. In other words, rate cuts will merely lessen the degree of policy restriction, in our view, rather than shift to an outright loosening stance.

• This also suggests that rate cuts will be more gradual than if central banks were responding to adverse shocks. Indeed, we see 25bp increments as the norm.
