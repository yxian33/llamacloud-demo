

# ESG Dashboard - Responsible Goodness

## Central Framework
At the core of the ESG strategy is **Responsible goodness**, supported by four strategic pillars arranged in a circular framework:
- **Growth** (Economic) - Red segment
- **Governance** - Yellow segment  
- **Resources** (Environmental) - Green segment
- **People** (Social) - Teal segment

## Key ESG Initiatives and Performance Metrics

### People (Social)
* Top Quartile in FMCG India in engagement score
* 2.3 Lac beneficiaries through BNF Vs 1.98 Lac in 21-22
* Planning a marketing campaign **"Har Pocket Dustbin"** which shall be on preventing littering in public places

### Resources (Environmental)
* **Renewable Energy** roadmap in place to increase share to **57% of total energy by FY 25-26**
* Initiated **Supplier's ESG assessment for FY 23-24** covering 100% of suppliers. To be completed by Q4'24.

### Governance
* S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment ESG score: **49**
* **CDP Climate Change Score** – Improved from **"C"** last year to **"B"** in 2023
* **ESG Data Management Tool** covering high level KPI dashboard & other reporting **under development**

## ESG Construct - 4 Strategic Pillars

### Framework Structure:
* **Growth** (Economic)
* **Governance** 
* **Resources** (Environmental)
* **People** (Social)

### Implementation Hierarchy:
* The 4 pillars are supported by **8 levers**
* The 8 levers split into **26 programs**
* **Programs with yearly KPIs & targets**
* **3-year Strategy Plan** in place
* Performance on KPIs to be part of **public disclosure** & **individual performance ratings**
