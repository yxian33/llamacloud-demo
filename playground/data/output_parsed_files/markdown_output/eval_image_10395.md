- **Forward P/E** (Red line): Actual Forward Price-to-Earnings ratio

- **+1.0sd** (Dashed gray line): One standard deviation above mean
- **-1.0sd** (Dashed gray line): One standard deviation below mean  
- **+2.0sd** (Dotted gray line): Two standard deviations above mean
- **-2.0sd** (Dotted gray line): Two standard deviations below mean
