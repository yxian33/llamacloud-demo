
# Clinical Trial Design

## Study Parameters

**180 patients**

**Total Duration: 26 weeks**

### Independent adjudication committee

### Stratified for
* IMNM
* ASyS  
* DM
* Severity of muscle weakness (MMT)

### Eligibility criteria
* Minimal muscle weakness
* No malignancies

**Adults** (both male and female patients)

## Treatment Arms

### IMNM
* 1000mg efgartigimod SC (+ SOC)
* Placebo SC (+ SOC)

### ASyS  
* 1000mg efgartigimod SC (+ SOC)
* Placebo SC (+ SOC)

### DM
* 1000mg efgartigimod SC (+ SOC)
* Placebo SC (+ SOC)

## Adaptive Enrichment

**Interim analysis**

**Independent DSMB**

* Subpopulation adjustment
* Sample size re-estimation

## Proposed Endpoints

### Primary
Response based on Total Improvement Score (TIS)
*ACR/EULAR endorsed*

### Key Secondary
* Mean change and duration in TIS
* Quality of Life
* Individual Core Set Measures TIS
  * MMT-8 score
  * Physician Global Assessment
  * Patient Global Assessment
  * HAQ-Disability Index score
  * Extramuscular Global Assessment
  * Muscle enzyme serum level
