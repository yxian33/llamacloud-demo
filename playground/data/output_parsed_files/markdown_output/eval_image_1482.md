**Chart Details:**
- **Y-axis:** Basis points (bp), ranging from -15 to 25
- **X-axis:** Days from election day, ranging from -100 to 100
- **Blue line:** 10y gilt-Bunds spread
- **Green line:** 2s10s yield curve spread
- **Orange vertical line:** Election called
- **Red vertical line:** Election day (8 June 2017)

**Key Observations:**
- Both spreads showed significant volatility during the campaign period
- The 2s10s spread peaked around 21bp shortly after the election was called
- Post-election, both measures generally declined and remained in negative territory
- The 10y gilt-Bunds spread showed a notable recovery toward the end of the observation period
