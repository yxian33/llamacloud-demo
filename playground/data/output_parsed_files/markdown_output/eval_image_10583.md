

## Market segment

* A data center is a physical facility that organizations utilize to house their critical application & data

* Comprises routers, switches, firewalls, servers, storage systems, controllers

**Data Center Hierarchy:**
- Captive
- Colocation  
- Enterprise DC (Small & Med)

## Future trends

### Technical Trends

* SKID solution – Less footprint & more capacity reduced go live time

* Communicable breakers / Equipment

* Scalable & flexible solutions on need basis

* Sustainable & eco friendly products & solutions

### Growth drivers

* Data Centers classified under Infrastructure facilities by Govt & Tax incentives for CAPEX

* Data Center Parks: RBI's data localization norms & Data Protection policy: Bill for local data storage
