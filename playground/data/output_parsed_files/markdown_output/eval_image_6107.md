## Economic Periods and Market Drivers

**Global recession spreading to India leading to economy impact** (FY08-FY09)

**Low ownership cost, Better freight management, above average monsoon** (FY10-FY11)

**Downcycle: Higher interest rate, rising fuel and vehicle prices, dampened consumer sentiment** (FY12-FY15)

**Upcycle: Favorable diesel rates, BSIV transition, Good monsoon, increase in infra development** (FY16-FY19)

**Weak consumer sentiment, Postponement of vehicle purchase during Covid** (FY20-FY21)

**Pent up demand boosted sales in FY 22 and FY23, growth tapered down in FY24** (FY22-FY24YTD)
