

# Brent Oil Price Market Cycles (1999-2024)

**Market cycle:**
- **Super Bull** (Blue)
- **Weak Bull** (Light Orange) 
- **Bear** (Red)

| Time Period | Price Range (USD/b) | Market Cycle      | Key Characteristics                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1999-2001   | 10-35               | Super Bull / Bear | Initial rise from \~10 to \~35, followed by decline        |
| 2002-2008   | 20-145              | Super Bull        | Strong upward trend, peak at \~145 in 2008                 |
| 2008-2009   | 145-40              | Bear              | Sharp decline from peak to \~40                            |
| 2009-2011   | 40-125              | Weak Bull         | Recovery and rise to \~125                                 |
| 2011-2014   | 90-125              | Bear              | High volatility, generally declining from \~125            |
| 2014-2016   | 110-30              | Bear              | Major decline from \~110 to \~30                           |
| 2016-2018   | 30-85               | Weak Bull         | Recovery from lows to \~85                                 |
| 2018-2020   | 85-20               | Bear              | Decline with dramatic drop to \~20 in 2020                 |
| 2020-2022   | 20-130              | Super Bull        | Sharp recovery from 2020 lows to \~130                     |
| 2022-2024   | 130-80              | Bear / Weak Bull  | Decline from peaks, transitioning to weak bull around \~80 |


*Y-axis: Brent oil (USD/b)*  
*X-axis: Years (99 = 1999, 24 = 2024)*
