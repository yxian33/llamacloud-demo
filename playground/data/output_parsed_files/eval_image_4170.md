- Major peak around 2006 followed by sharp decline during 2008-2011 financial crisis
- Strong recovery and growth from 2012-2022
- 2023 shows actual prices declining while VAR forecast predicted continued growth
- The forecast appears to have overestimated 2023 housing prices by approximately $50,000
