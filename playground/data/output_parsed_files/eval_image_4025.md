

**Mixed picture for near-term growth:** Recent business and consumer surveys have been mixed. While the INSEE business climate has edged higher since November, it remains below the historical average and consistent with subdued GDP growth. The composite PMI has barely stabilised, at low levels (47.7). On a more positive note, industrial production rose markedly in December, while confidence has been recovering among consumers and retailers.

All considered, we still think this is consistent with growth at 0.1% q/q in Q1 after two consecutive quarters of quasi-stagnation.

**Disinflation underway:** France's inflation has been decelerating at a slower rate than eurozone inflation in the past six months, partly due to the lagged increase in regulated energy prices. Nevertheless, disinflation is well underway.

With an output gap estimated to be slightly negative at the start of 2024, we think inflation will fall slightly below 2% in H2 2024 (Figure 7).
