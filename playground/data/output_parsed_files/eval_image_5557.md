## Key Observations
- The EV/EBITDA ratio hit a low of approximately 11.0 in late 2019
- There was a significant recovery starting in 2020, reaching the post-COVID average level
- The ratio remained relatively stable around the post-COVID average through 2022
- A sharp increase occurred in 2023, reaching approximately 27.0, well above the +1 standard deviation level
- The post-COVID average (~21.0) is notably higher than the pre-COVID average (~17.3)
