
# Economic Indicators: NFIB Job Difficulty and Unemployment Rate (1975-2023)

| Indicator                                               | Description                                                | Scale/Notes                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| NFIB: % of firms with 1 or more jobs hard to fill (lhs) | Percentage of firms reporting difficulty filling positions | Left axis: 0.0 - 55.0             |
| US Unemployment Rate (rhs, 6m lag, inverted)            | Unemployment rate with 6-month lag, inverted scale         | Right axis: 0.0 - 10.0 (inverted) |
| Latest                                                  | Current/most recent value reference line                   | Dashed horizontal line at \~40.0  |
| Recession                                               | Economic recession periods                                 | Gray vertical bars                |


## Key Observations from Chart Data:

**Recession Periods (Gray bars):**
- Early 1980s recession
- Early 1990s recession  
- Early 2000s recession
- 2008-2009 Great Recession
- 2020 COVID-19 recession

**Data Trends:**
- NFIB job difficulty percentage ranges from approximately 5% to 52% over the time period
- US Unemployment rate (inverted) shows inverse correlation with job difficulty
- Both series show cyclical patterns corresponding to economic cycles
- Recent period (2020-2023) shows sharp movements in both indicators
- The "Latest" reference line appears at approximately 40% level
- Peak job difficulty periods often coincide with or precede recession periods
- The inverted unemployment rate generally moves in the same direction as job difficulty, indicating the expected inverse relationship between unemployment and job market tightness

**Time Period:** 1975 - 2023 (approximately 48 years of data)

**Chart Type:** Dual-axis time series with overlaid recession indicators and reference lines
