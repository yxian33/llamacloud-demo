
# Exiobase vs Wunderpus V2.13 Comparison

## Exiobase

- Exiobase is a global, multi-regional input-output table.

- 44 countries, 5 Regions

- 200 products & services

- 163 industries

- The hybrid supply-use and input-output tables of EXIOBASE are available for the year 2011

## Wunderpus V2.13

- Input-output table based on Exiobase 3 but extended to be more granular

- 259 regions

- + 2,200 products & services

- > 1,200 sectors

- Yearly updated with 41 different inflation rates
