## Time Series Overview (Jan 2015 - Mar 2024)

The P/E ratio (red line) shows significant volatility over the 9-year period:

**Key Periods:**
- **2015-2016**: High volatility with multiple peaks reaching above +2 SD (~30-33)
- **2017**: Gradual decline from elevated levels to around average
- **2018-2019**: Relatively stable around average to slightly below average levels
- **Early 2020**: Sharp decline to lowest point (~12), well below -2 SD
- **2020-2021**: Strong recovery with peak reaching above +1 SD (~30)
- **2022-2024**: Gradual decline and stabilization around average to slightly below average levels

**Y-axis Scale**: 10 to 35 (x)
**X-axis**: Monthly intervals from Jan-15 through Mar-24

The chart demonstrates the P/E ratio's tendency to oscillate around statistical norms, with notable periods of extreme deviation during market stress (2020 crash) and subsequent recovery phases.
