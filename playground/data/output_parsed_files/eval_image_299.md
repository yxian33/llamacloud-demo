## Chart Details

**Y-Axis Scale:** 0.8x to 1.9x
**Time Period:** January 2010 to March 2024
**Data Frequency:** Monthly observations

## Key Observations

- **Philippines** showed the strongest performance during 2013-2015, reaching nearly 1.9x
- **Thailand** demonstrated the most consistent long-term growth trajectory from 2010-2020
- All countries experienced peak performance around 2013-2014 period
- **Malaysia** and **Singapore** show similar patterns with gradual decline post-2013
- Recent years (2020-2024) show increased volatility across all countries, particularly **Thailand** and **Philippines**
