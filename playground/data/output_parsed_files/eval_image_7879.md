
# Profiling Pop Mart's target audience

| **Age**                                       | 15-40                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gender**                                    | Female 70-80%, male 20-30% (slightly increasing contribution)                                                                                                                                                 |
| **City**                                      | 25% in tier-1 cities, 25% in tier-2 cities                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Income**                                    | 90% with CNY 8-20K per month                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Top reasons for pop toys purchase**         | 1) surprise due to blind boxes uncertainties, 2) sense of satisfaction after collecting whole series, 3) sense of identity with pop toy community and other emotional value, 4) appropriate gifts for friends |
| **Top reasons for buying PopMart's products** | 1) proprietary IPs, with Skullpanda, Molly and Dimoo being the most popular, 2) frequent product/series refresh, 3) high quality design of the series                                                         |
