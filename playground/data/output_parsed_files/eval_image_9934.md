

**Negative watch:** Among the main rating agencies, only Standard & Poor's has a negative watch on French sovereign ratings. Its next review is planned on May 31.

The agencies' rating drivers pay attention to the growth and deficit trajectory in the coming quarters. They will also gauge the ability of the French government to take action to limit any slippage.

Against this backdrop, the outcome of the new spending review, which is supposed to find EUR12bn structural savings for 2025, will be particularly important, in our view.
