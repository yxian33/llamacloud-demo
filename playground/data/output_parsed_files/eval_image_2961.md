

| Alternative paths for early election    | Procedure                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Confidence vote triggered by chancellor | The president can dissolve parliament within 21 days if the chancellor fails to gain majority support in a confidence vote triggered by the chancellor himself (unless a new chancellor is elected in the meantime with majority support).                                                                     |
| Constructive confidence vote            | MPs can express no confidence in the chancellor through a 'constructive' confidence vote. In this case, they would also need to vote for a new chancellor. If the newly elected chancellor is elected only on the basis of a relative but not absolute majority, the president can opt to dissolve parliament. |


If parliament is dissolved, new elections must take place within 60 days.
