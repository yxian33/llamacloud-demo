

**Cutting red tape:** In the wake of recent farmers' protests, the new government has announced an initiative to relax rules and ease the regulatory burden across the board.

In particular, the government wants to quicken the delivery of construction permits for residential or industrial projects. France has the longest construction permit process among large advanced economies, according to the World Bank.

**Low expectations:** While the government's focus on cutting red tape is welcome, in our view, the results of this new initiative are uncertain.

Over the past decades, there have been numerous simplification programmes, most recently when Macron was economy minister in 2015. Despite past efforts, France continues to have one of the most tightly regulated product markets among advanced economies, according to OECD indicators.

We think one of the main problems to solve is the number of different administrative layers that exist in France. But reforming the administrative structure is politically difficult, and in our view unlikely, given current division in the National Assembly.
