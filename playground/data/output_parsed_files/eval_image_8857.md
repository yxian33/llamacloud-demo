

## Segment Insights

* Indian electronics market is expected to grow at CAGR of 30% to reach $300bn by FY26

* Segmentation of Indian electronics market

| Segment                      | Percentage |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- |
| Cell Phones                  | 40%        |
| Consumer electronics         | 14%        |
| PCBA & Electronic components | 13%        |
| Industrial electronics       | 13%        |
| Auto electronics             | 8%         |
| Strategic electronics        | 5%         |
| Computer HW                  | 4%         |
| LEDs                         | 3%         |


## Key Drivers

* Make in India and Digital India initiatives by Govt. of India

* Semicon India Program for setting up Semiconductor Fabs, Display Fabs, Compound Semiconductors, OSAT/ ATMP, Design Linked Incentive

* Schemes for Promotion of Electronics Manufacturing: PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing and IT Hardware, SPECS, EMC 2.0

* Rollout of 5G and Industrial use of IoT technology

* Large consumer base with increasing penetration of electronic products

* Third largest startup ecosystem in India
