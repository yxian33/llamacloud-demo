**Chart Elements:**
- **ECB QE**: Light blue/cyan shaded regions indicating European Central Bank Quantitative Easing periods
- **FED QE**: Gray shaded region indicating Federal Reserve Quantitative Easing period
- **EURUSD 6m**: Red line showing 6-month EUR/USD exchange rate data
- **Reference Level**: Horizontal dashed red line at approximately 6.5 level

*Dashed line indicates most recent level*

**Axis Information:**
- Left Y-axis: Scale from 0 to 14
- Right Y-axis: Scale from 0 to 1
- X-axis: Time period from January 2014 to January 2024
