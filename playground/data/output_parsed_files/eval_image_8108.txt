                                     Election timing scenarios

Base case: October/November 2024                                                          BNPP subjective probability 659/
 Prime Minister Rishi Sunak has stated his "working assumption" is that the election will
 take place in the second half of this year; and we think considerations discussed in
 greater detail on the next page make October or November the most likely months in H2.
 An autumn election would take advantage of improving economic conditions and give        Betting market implied probability  71%
 time for the government to close the current polling gap (see section Qpinion polls)

 Main risk: 2 May 2024                                                                    BNPP subjective probability         309
 We cannot rule out a spring election; on the same day as local elections.
  An early budget (6 March 2024) leaves scope for the prime minister to call an election if
  policy announcements lead to a narrowing in the polls
 In addition; legislative or leadership challenges could prompt a spring election:         Betting market implied probability 159
