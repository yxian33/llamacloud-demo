

# Environmental Impact Assessment Framework

## Level 1: Data Sources
- Company reports
- Factset
- I/O Iceberg
- External Database

**Flow:** Input → Input/Output → Output

**Outcome:** Total Revenue breakdown into:
- Sales by Segment by Country 1
- Sales by Segment by Country 2

## Level 2: Environmental Assessment Categories
- Life Cycle Assessment data bases
- Scientific literature

**Categories:**
- Land Use
- GHG emissions  
- Air Pollution
- Water Pollution

## Level 3: Impact Assessment
- GLOBIO
- Pressure-Impact Relationship

**Impact Categories:**
- Land Use Impact
- Climate Change Impact
- Air Pollution Impact
- Water Pollution Impact

## Level 4: Company-Based Footprint (CBF) Indicators
- Indicator: avoid company size biases

**CBF Metrics:**
- CBF Capital Employed
- CBF Enterprise Value
- CBF Main services
