
# El Niño

| Region/Component                 | El Niño Effects                   | Direction/Flow                    |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Americas                         | Increased convection              | Upward air movement               |
| Trade Winds                      | Trade winds drop                  | Weakened easterly flow            |
| Ocean Surface                    | Warm surface water flows eastward | Eastward current toward Americas  |
| Pacific Ocean Temperature (East) | Warmer than normal                | Elevated sea surface temperatures |
| Pacific Ocean Temperature (West) | Cooler than normal                | Reduced sea surface temperatures  |
| South East Asia and Australia    | Reduced atmospheric circulation   | Weakened convection patterns      |
