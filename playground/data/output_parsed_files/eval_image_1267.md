* **Economic environment particularly uncertain**: EM inflation prints continue to show a bias for downward surprises, adding to uncertainty about continued disinflation, given resilience in US economic data.

* **Data matter most when central banks are data dependent**: Even those EM central banks that have already embarked on rate cuts have done so conveying some degree of data dependency. There remains a general preference for policy optionality, which we think facilitates the assessment of high-frequency data to measure the impact of monetary transmission.

* **Bumpier path ahead**: Resilience in activity should not prevent lower inflation, keeping EM in the 'expansion' quadrant of our BERT framework, but it could make disinflation bumpier and more uncertain than we previously assumed. We think the persistence and pace of EM rate cuts face an acute trade-off between currency performance and growth in H2 2024.

* **Keep cutting for now**: We continue to see major EM central banks reaching their inflation targets by Q4 2025, keeping cuts in play for now. In the CE3, however, re-acceleration in inflation should slow the pace of cuts from Q2.
