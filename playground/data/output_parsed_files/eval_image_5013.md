
# Change vs. 1995

| -------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| US             | Teal/Dark Green | Started near 0% in 1995, remained relatively stable until \~2001, then showed gradual decline to around -5% by 2003, followed by steady increase reaching approximately 25-30% by 2019 |
| Australia      | Orange          | Started near 0% in 1995, showed initial decline to around -5% by 1999, then steady increase peaking around 15% in 2003-2005, followed by decline and leveling off near 0% by 2019      |
| UK             | Light Blue/Gray | Started near 0% in 1995, remained relatively stable until \~2003, then showed steady decline reaching approximately -10% to -15% by 2019                                               |
| Canada         | Dark Blue       | Started near 0% in 1995, remained stable until \~2001, showed some fluctuation with slight increases around 2003-2005, then gradual decline to approximately -5% to -10% by 2019       |


**Time Period:** January 1995 to January 2019 (24-year span)

**Scale:** Percentage change ranging from -15% to +40% relative to 1995 baseline

**Key Observations:**
- All countries started at approximately 0% change in 1995 (baseline year)
- US showed the most dramatic positive growth, especially after 2005
- Australia showed early growth followed by decline back to baseline
- UK and Canada both showed overall declining trends, particularly in later years
- Most significant divergence occurred after 2005, with countries showing distinctly different trajectories
