
**Resilient corporates:** We think the interest rate shock will also impact business investment, but to a lesser extent than residential investment. French corporates enjoyed relatively high profitability during the pandemic, around 1% above historical levels. This is partly due to the government cutting production taxes in its recovery plan and partly to the freeze in regulated electricity prices for small companies.

High profitability gives French corporates some room to cushion a higher capital cost. Business surveys confirm a resilient financial situation. For instance, SMEs' opinion on their cash balances remained above average in H2 2023.

**Higher investment trends:** French business investment has been on an uptrend in the past decade (see page 13). We think the factors that underpin this positive trend will limit the downturn and help investment grow again from H2 2024.

In addition, the government also continues to supporting investment trends through its France 2030 plan, consisting of EUR54bn funds to technological and industrial sectors. In 2023, EUR21bn of this plan had already been committed.
