## Key Observations
- The Bank P/B Ratio experienced significant volatility during the COVID-19 period (2020)
- The ratio dropped to its lowest point around 1.8 in March 2020
- Post-COVID recovery brought the ratio back above pre-pandemic levels temporarily
- Current levels (2023) are stabilizing around 2.7, slightly below the pre-COVID average
- The post-COVID 3-year average is lower than the pre-COVID average, indicating a structural shift in valuation
