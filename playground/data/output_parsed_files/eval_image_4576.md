• **Challenging outlook in China:** The economy continues to face myriad cyclical and structural headwinds, while the policy response looks to be conservative.

• A widening negative output gap would generate greater deflationary pressure, leading the economy to broad-based deflation, in our view.

• A negative growth shock from China would be disinflationary to the rest of the world, through reducing the import demand for commodities and exporting more products at cheaper prices.

• **Latam:** Activity will be propped up by stronger links to the US, we expect, coupled with positive real income effects from lower inflation and interest rates.

• **EM Asia:** We think base effects create scope for some catch-up. South Korea will find support in the pickup of semi-conductor demand, in our view, and India in strong domestic growth drivers.

• **CEEMEA:** The CE3 remains supported by real incomes. We see strong headwinds in Turkey (restrictive rates for longer) and Egypt (EGP devaluation) and structural issues to stifle South Africa's growth potential.
