## Analysis

• **Turning point in capex**: The anticipation of policy rate cuts and improving growth expectations has contributed to easing financial conditions in large advanced economies. This creates a more favourable environment for business investment, in our view.

• In addition, we believe higher growth in advanced economies will reduce the adverse impact of higher borrowing costs on corporate debt refinancing.

• There are already signs of a pickup in the global capex cycle. For instance, export orders registered in Taiwan and South Korea have edged higher in recent months, pointing to a rise in global trade after several quarters of decline. The recent rise in global semiconductor sales supports this view.

• Finally, confidence is one channel through which supply shocks can have a positive economic impact. In the US, we think the return of animal spirits could encourage investment trends. The recent rise in CEO confidence suggests this is about to happen.
