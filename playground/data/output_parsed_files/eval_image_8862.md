* The Congressional Budget Office made significant upward revisions to its population growth estimates and projections in its January 2024 demographic outlook, largely due to higher immigration.

* Heightened attention on immigration flows ahead of November's election could result in stricter border-control measures — a downside risk to the positive labor-supply shock (see page 27 for more).

* Ongoing rebalancing in the job market, as signaled by normalizing job quits and job openings, suggests that growth in the Fed's preferred wage metric — the Employment Cost Index — should continue to moderate in 2024 and 2025.

* Our updated projection is for GDP growth of about 2.1% y/y in 2024 on a Q4/Q4 basis (revised from 1.0%), led by resilient consumer spending and an upgrade to our business investment forecast.

* In 2025, we project growth at 1.9% (revised from 1.8%). Uncertainty is high, as fiscal policy could shift after the election (for more, see page 16).
