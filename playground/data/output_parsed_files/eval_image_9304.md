
# By 2027

* Deposit growth CAGR will be ~23-25% with focus on individual and retail deposits

* Asset book growth will be in line with deposit growth with higher growth from businesses with higher RoA

* Asset quality to remain within range; PCR incl. tech write-off would be around 65% - 70%

* Unsecured book will be 15-20% with MFI book capped at 10% of AUM (including securitisation)

* Annual credit cost on MFI book to be taken @ 2.5-3% every year

* Increasing mix of high yield, high RoA portfolio assets to ~75.25

* Endeavour to achieve RoA of 1.8%
