
# Forward EV/EBITDA Time Series Analysis

| Period              | Forward EV/EBITDA Range | Notable Features                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Jan 2013 - Mar 2014 | 11.0 - 13.5             | High volatility, values above mean        |
| Apr 2014 - Dec 2015 | 7.0 - 10.5              | Declining trend, approaching mean         |
| Jan 2016 - Dec 2016 | 8.0 - 10.0              | Recovery period, fluctuating around mean  |
| Jan 2017 - Sep 2017 | 10.0 - 12.8             | Sharp increase, peak values               |
| Oct 2017 - Dec 2018 | 8.0 - 11.5              | Gradual decline from peak                 |
| Jan 2019 - Mar 2020 | 6.0 - 8.5               | Below mean, declining trend               |
| Apr 2020            | 3.0                     | Sharp drop to minimum value               |
| May 2020 - Dec 2021 | 6.0 - 9.5               | Recovery and stabilization                |
| Jan 2022 - Feb 2024 | 6.0 - 9.5               | Continued fluctuation around lower levels |


**Statistical Reference Lines:**
- Mean = 8.9 (gray dashed line)
- +1 Standard Deviation = 10.7 (black dashed line)  
- -1 Standard Deviation = 7.1 (pink dashed line)

**Key Observations:**
- The Forward EV/EBITDA ratio shows significant volatility over the 11-year period
- Values ranged from a low of approximately 3.0 (April 2020) to highs above 13.0 (early 2013)
- The metric spent considerable time above the mean in 2013-2014 and 2016-2017
- A notable sharp decline occurred in April 2020, likely related to market disruption
- Recent years (2020-2024) show values generally clustering around or below the long-term mean
