* We expect GDP growth to slow to 4.5% in 2024 from 5.2% last year, with risks skewed to the downside. Although the National People's Congress kept this year's official growth target at "about 5%", we think the modest policy stimulus mapped out looks insufficient to fulfil this target.

* Downbeat consumer sentiment and the property sector slump seem to have formed a negative feedback loop.

* A cyclical upswing in exports is likely to be a silver lining amid the slowdown.

* We expect the headline CPI to decline by 0.1% y/y in 2024, mainly on food price deflation. Food prices will fall by 2.3% this year, we forecast, due to weak demand for protein and increased supply of most food categories after the pandemic.

* Continued deflationary pass-through from the PPI, labour market slack and weak consumer demand are likely to keep core inflation roughly flat at 0.7%.

* The PBoC normally looks through food-induced inflation wobbles. We expect it to maintain a cautious easing stance and cut interest rates by 25bp this year.
