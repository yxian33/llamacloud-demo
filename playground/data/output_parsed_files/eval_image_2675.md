## How was this calculated?

### Identifiable Capex Methodology:
**(a)** Announced US renewables per BNEF's database. Cancelled / abandoned excluded. Results in over 500 GW large scale assets. **(b)** DG added at 5 GW p.a. for 5yr (vast majority demand 'at risk'). **(c)** Module manufacturing plants are included at announced or typical capex. **(d)** 2.3 mtpa identified green hydrogen projects per BNEF, at $8 mn per TPD cost. **(e)** Approx. $100 bn CCS/CCUS announced as of late '22. **(f)** Unit capex: Solar $1.25/W, ESS $1/W, Onshore $2/W, Offshore $4.5/W, DG $3/W, Other Clean Power (geo, biomass, etc) avg. $3/W. **(g)** Power assets split Red/Blue based on actuals, h2/CCS based on TX focus. **(h)** Purple states included in "red".

### Realistic Capex Methodology:
**(a)** For all assets, anything with permits or financing secured included **(b)** Solar / ESS / Onshore, 50% announced non-permitted assets included **(c)** Offshore, nothing included w/o permits/financing **(d)** DG, all demand assumed 'at risk' included **(e)** 40% of h2 and CCS assets assumed realistic (1 in 4 likely, improved by hubs and non-IRA subsidies) **(f)** Half of module plants excluded, which roughly corresponds with non-Tier 1 / FSLR. **(g)** Unit capex assumptions same as scenario on the left. **(h)** Purple states included in "red". **Note:** Not all investments included in the overall analysis, especially committed grid investment needed to accommodate identified assets.
