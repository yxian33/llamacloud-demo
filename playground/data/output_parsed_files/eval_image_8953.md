## Polar Projection Map of Ancient Earth Seas

This polar projection map shows the distribution of ancient seas and climate patterns across Earth. The map displays various seas and water bodies with their geographical locations marked by latitude and longitude coordinates.

### Major Seas and Water Bodies Shown:

**Arctic and Northern Seas:**
- Spitsbergen
- Jan Mayen
- Greenland Sea
- Boreal Sea
- Barents Sea
- Kara Sea
- Laptev Sea
- East Siberian Sea
- Chukchi Sea
- Beaufort Sea

**Atlantic Region:**
- Labrador Sea
- North Sea
- Faroe Sea

**Other Regions:**
- Turgai Sea
- Various unnamed water bodies in blue (indicating wetter conditions)
- Several areas marked in yellow (indicating drier tendency)

The map includes a coordinate grid system with latitude and longitude markings at regular intervals (30°, 60°, etc.), and shows the continental outlines as they existed during this ancient time period. The projection is centered on the North Pole, providing a comprehensive view of the northern hemisphere's ancient geography and climate patterns.
