The chart displays the Forward P/E (Price-to-Earnings) ratio over a 9-year period from February 2015 to February 2024. The red line shows the actual Forward P/E values, which fluctuated between approximately 7.8 and 13.4 during this period.

Key observations from the chart:
- The Forward P/E ratio remained relatively stable around 11.5-12.5 from 2015 through early 2018
- There were two notable peaks reaching above 13.0 in 2017 and early 2018
- A dramatic drop occurred around early 2020, falling to approximately 7.8 (likely corresponding to market volatility)
- The ratio recovered to around 12.0 in 2020-2021
- From 2021 onwards, there has been a general declining trend, ending near 10.0 by February 2024
- The mean value of 11.3 is represented by a gray dashed horizontal line
- The +1 standard deviation (12.4) and -1 standard deviation (10.3) levels are shown as black and orange dashed lines respectively
