## Chart Analysis

The chart displays the 3-month difference in US multi-asset fund equity beta from 2019 to 2024. The data shows:

- **Volatility Pattern**: Consistent fluctuations between approximately -0.3 and +0.15 for most of the period
- **2024 Spike**: A dramatic increase reaching approximately 0.42, the highest point in the entire time series
- **Threshold Level**: Green shading below approximately -0.1 indicates a significant threshold or benchmark level
- **Reference Comparison**: The 10th percentile expanding window provides context for the main series performance
- **Overall Trend**: Generally oscillating around zero with periods both above and below the baseline
